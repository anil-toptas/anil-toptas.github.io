Neon Trails

Connect the dots. Fill the grid.

Noktaları birleştir. Izgarayı doldur.

Neon Trails is a relaxing puzzle game for iPhone. Connect each pair of same-colored dots with a path so that every square of the grid is filled. Paths can’t cross — and drawing into another color’s path cuts it.

  • 100 levels, from 5×5 to 9×9
  • Daily puzzles: 3 new puzzles every day
  • Weekly puzzles: 10 puzzles up to 10×10 every week
  • Daily and weekly streaks
  • Hints when you get stuck
  • Plays fully offline · no account needed
  • English and Turkish
